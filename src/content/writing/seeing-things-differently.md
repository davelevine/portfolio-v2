## Summary

This should serve as a reminder that even the most basic things can be interpreted differently by different people. Always be clear with requirements and don't assume anyone knows what you're talking about.

## Interpretations

![Ten-panel cartoon of a tree swing project. How the customer explained it: a three-seat swing. How the project leader understood it: a swing looped around the trunk. How the analyst designed it: a swing hung under a trunk cut away and propped up. How the programmer wrote it: ropes tied around the trunk, seat on the ground. How the business consultant described it: an armchair on a swing in a sunburst. How the project was documented: nothing. What operations installed: a single rope. How the customer was billed: a roller coaster. How it was supported: a stump.
